№ 01 — Manifesto
We believe the internet has flattened taste. That distribution rewards volume over voice, and that the work that matters rarely trends. Atelier is a slow place for fast minds — a salon, not a feed. It exists to introduce you to the people whose work you'd be proud to share a margin with.
